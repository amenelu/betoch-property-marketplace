# Betoch monetization priorities

Basic property search should remain free. Revenue should come from professionals receiving visibility, workflow value or qualified demand—not from charging buyers to see ordinary listings.

## 1. Featured and boosted listings

Owners, brokers and developers pay for time-limited `featured`, `search_boost`, `homepage` or `neighborhood_boost` placement. Every paid result must display `Sponsored`. The MVP starts with admin-controlled promotions and configurable prices; payment processing remains out of scope.

Why first: it is simple to explain, uses the existing property inventory and can be sold manually before payment integration.

## 2. Broker and agency subscriptions

Configurable Free, Pro and Agency plans can control listing limits, analytics, team access, profile presentation, lead management and included boosts. Prices and entitlements belong in database/admin configuration, never hard-coded in the UI.

Why second: recurring revenue aligns with brokers keeping availability and listing quality current.

## 3. Developer project packages

Developers pay for project profiles, unit-type inventory, construction updates, homepage campaigns, neighborhood sponsorship and lead analytics. All campaigns are labeled.

Why high-value: one developer relationship can supply many units and a larger marketing budget.

## 4. Qualified leads

Charge only for agreed lead events such as a completed inquiry, viewing request, verified phone/WhatsApp introduction or booking request. A page view is never a billable lead. Lead definitions, consent, duplicate handling and dispute rules must be explicit.

Why later: lead quality must be proven before charging, or brokers will distrust the product.

## 5. Listing services

Offer optional photography, measurement, listing preparation, translation and manual verification visits. These create original authorized inventory while generating service revenue.

Why useful at launch: Betoch can sell operational value before it has large traffic.

## 6. Property-service referrals

Clearly labeled referrals to movers, cleaners, photographers, internet providers, security installers, interior designers and property managers can generate referral or advertising revenue. Providers require vetting and transparent placement rules.

## 7. Market intelligence

After Betoch has sufficient licensed, normalized historical data, sell professional reports and dashboards covering asking-price trends, price per square metre, listing duration and neighborhood comparisons. Outputs must be described as marketplace asking-price intelligence, not official values or automated valuations.

## Recommended rollout

| Stage | Revenue products |
|---|---|
| Launch | Free founding-broker onboarding, paid listing preparation/photography where viable, manually administered featured placements |
| Initial traction | Pro/Agency subscriptions, developer project packages, sponsored campaigns |
| Proven demand | Qualified-lead packages and service-provider referrals |
| Data maturity | Professional property-intelligence subscriptions and reports |

Do not add mortgages, escrow, transaction payments, commissions on property sales, government verification claims or automated valuation in the MVP.
